Mike Klein

"I appreciate photography as a way to look carefully at the natural world. Too often we see the world through our ideas about it or miss it altogether in the rush of our lives. Through the camera lens we can focus, pay attention to beauty, find surprising combinations of light and shadow, texture and color. The photograph can preserve our feelings or inspire new ones. The North Shore of Lake Superior is a favorite place to capture images, but even local parks and the backyard can offer surprises."
